# Math

Inline formulas sit on the line: Euler's identity $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$, the
fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, a root $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ and Greek letters $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$.
Prices like $5 and $10 stay plain text.

Display formulas get their own line:

$$
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}
$$

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

| Name      | Formula                          |
| --------- | -------------------------------- |
| Pythagoras | $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$               |
| Quadratic | $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ |

A matrix:

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}
$$

Broken math is shown as code: $\frac{1$.
